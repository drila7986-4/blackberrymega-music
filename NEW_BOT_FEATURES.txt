BLACKBERRY MUSIC - NEW BOT

DM /start shows the full feature menu immediately in a styled Blackberry Music panel.

MUSIC
/play <song/link>
/vplay <video/link>
/song <name/link>
/pause /resume /skip /stop /queue /playlist
/autoplay on|off
/musicstatus /vcstatus
Fast acknowledgement is sent before download work starts.
Autoplay moves to the next queued track when StreamEnded is received.

BROADCAST (OWNER/SUDO)
/setuser, /unsetuser
/setgroup, /unsetgroup
/setlist
/broadcast <text>
/broadcast groups <text>
/broadcast dm <text>
/broadcast all <text>

HIDDEN WORD
/hiddenword
/hw
/word10
10 sequential hidden-word rounds. A player who solves all 10 first receives 3000 coins.
/gamecancel or /hiddenstop cancels the active game.

COINS
/daily = 500 coins once every 24 hours
/coins = balance
/topcoins = leaderboard
/gamescore = game score + coins

SECURITY
Do not upload .env. Keep BOT_TOKEN, API_HASH, SESSION_STRING, API_ID, OWNER_ID and MONGO_URI in Render environment variables.
